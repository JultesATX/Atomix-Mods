Sonic 3 Legacy Edition

A complete game overhaul mod featuring new mechanics and a renovated campaign.

About the Mod

After the events of Sonic 3 & Knuckles, an uncertain peace falls over Angel Island while Dr. Eggman remains in an unknown location. In his absence, Metal Sonic has emerged following a final directive from his creator, evolving into his Neo form driven by an unbreakable loyalty. This new power has triggered a temporal distortion that fractures reality; as the Master Emerald is affected by this energy, it has amplified the chaos, dragging Sonic and his friends into unpredictable temporal extremes. Unlike in past events, this time the Master Emerald has reacted to the crisis, finally awakening the power of the Super Emeralds as the only hope to stabilize time. Experience this adventure, face the distortion, and stop Neo Metal Sonic before the history of Angel Island is lost forever.
